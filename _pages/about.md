Lvfang Tao is a Senior Researcher at [Tencent HY](http://hunyuan.tencent.com/), focusing on Multimodal Reinforcement Learning. Prior to this role, he was a core contributor to the reinforcement-learning platform [AI Arena](https://aiarena.tencent.com/aiarena/en/), where he led algorithm exploration. He joined Tencent in 2022 after receiving his Master of Science degree from the [School of Electronic and Computer Engineering (SECE)](https://www.ece.pku.edu.cn/) at [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/). His work focuses on mid-training and post-training for large language models—including synthetic data, on-policy distillation, RLVR, and agentic reinforcement learning—as well as the development of RL environments and agents. Since 2019, he has led projects on from-scratch and continual pretraining across text, speech, and image modalities.

陶履方，2022 年毕业于北京大学信息工程学院并获得理学硕士学位，同年加入腾讯。现任 [腾讯混元](http://hunyuan.tencent.com/) 多模态强化学习高级研究员，此前曾担任 [腾讯开悟平台](https://aiarena.tencent.com/aiarena/zh/) 核心贡献者并负责算法探索工作。个人研究兴趣包括大模型的中训练与后训练（涉及合成数据、on-policy 蒸馏、RLVR、Tool-Use / Agentic RL 等议题），以及强化学习环境、智能体与平台系统的研发。自 2019 年起，他在文本、语音和图像等模态上积累了大规模从零预训练与增量预训练的经验。
